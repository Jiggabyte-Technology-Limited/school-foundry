# 🏫 School Foundry Offline — Bursar & Headteacher Quick-Start Guide
**Digital Public Good Edition • 100% Offline • Zero Monthly Fees**

---

## ⚡ Welcome to Day 1: Simple School Accounting That Never Fails

School Foundry is designed specifically for African schools operating without reliable internet. Everything runs on your office computer. Your financial records stay private on your premises, and subsidized children are protected from classroom exclusion.

---

### ⏱️ STEP 1: Installation & School Setup (5 Minutes)
1. **Run the Installer**: Double-click `SchoolFoundry-Setup.exe`. The app installs automatically in under 30 seconds—no internet or technical setup needed.
2. **First-Run Wizard**:
   - Enter your **School Name**, **Motto**, and **Contact Number**.
   - Select your operating currency (**ZMW / Kwacha**, **USD \$**, or **ZWG**).
   - Set up your Academic Year (e.g. *2026 Academic Year*) and Term dates (*Term 1, Term 2, Term 3*).
   - Enter your standard Grade Fee Structures (e.g. *Grade 8 Tuition: K1,200/term*).

---

### 🛡️ STEP 2: Enrolling Learners & Child Safeguarding (SDG 4 & 10)
1. Click **+ Add Learner** or use **Bulk Excel Import** to bring in your class rosters.
2. **Assigning Scholarships & Government Grants**:
   - If a student is sponsored by the **Constituency Development Fund (CDF)**, **Government Free Education Policy (FEP)**, **CAMFED**, **UNICEF**, or an **Internal School Scholarship**, open their profile.
   - Under **Child Safeguarding & Educational Subsidy**, select their provider and coverage (*100% Full, 50% Half, or Custom Amount*).
   - Check **"Prevent Academic Exclusion"**.
   - **The Safeguard**: The student will show a green `🛡️ SPONSORED` badge on class lists and accounts. Even if government disbursement is pending, **the student will NEVER appear on defaulter lockout lists or be barred from exams.**

---

### 🧾 STEP 3: Recording Payments & Issuing Receipts (2 Seconds)
1. In the **Payment Manager**, type the student's name or admission number.
2. Enter the amount paid, payment method (*Cash, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, Bank Slip*), and reference number.
3. Click **Record & Print Receipt**:
   - **Thermal Printer**: Instantly prints a 2-second receipt on standard 80mm paper roll.
   - **Standard Laser Printer**: Generates an A4 official payment receipt.
4. The student's ledger and balance update immediately.

---

### 📊 STEP 4: Daily Balancing & Reports
1. **Daily Cash Summary**: Click **Dashboard** at 16:00 to see total collections for the day broken down by cash vs. mobile money vs. bank deposits.
2. **Term Statements**: Click **Print Statement** on any student account to generate a complete, tamper-evident financial statement for parents.
3. **Class Defaulter Lists**: Filter by class to see true private fee arrears while sponsored learners remain protected.

---

## 🔒 THE 3 GOLDEN RULES OF OFFLINE RESILIENCE

| Rule | What to Do | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Friday USB Backup** | Every Friday before leaving the office, go to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Backup Database** and save a copy to your USB flash drive. | If your office laptop suffers power-surge damage during load-shedding, your data is safe and can be restored in 10 seconds on any replacement PC. |
| **2. Keep Your Void Key Secret** | Only the Headteacher or Senior Bursar should hold the 4-digit **Void Key**. | Prevents unauthorized payment deletions or cash discrepancies. |
| **3. Never Bar Protected Learners** | When generating exam entry rosters or attendance checks, look for the `🛡️ SPONSORED` badge. | Complies with national child safeguarding standards and UNICEF Digital Public Good principles. |
