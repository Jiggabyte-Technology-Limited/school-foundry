When paper ledgers get lost or chaotic spreadsheets fail, school finances collapse—and worst of all, vulnerable children are wrongfully barred from classrooms and exams due to unverified payment records."*

### Scene 3: Live Workflow & Financial Integrity (0:50 - 1:20)
- **Visuals:**
  - Screen recording of the **Payment Wizard**:
    1. Bursar searches a student (e.g., "Chanda Mulenga - Grade 9").
    2. Enters fee payment of 500 Kwacha.
    3. Clicks **"Process Payment"** $\rightarrow$ Instant thermal/PDF receipt pops up with a clean verification code.
  - Cut to **Student Accounts View** showing the real-time balance update and the immutable audit log entry.
- **Voiceover Script:**
  > *"In just three clicks, an administrator can record a fee payment, verify student enrollment, and issue an instant, professional receipt. Powered by a high-integrity local SQLite database with integer-cent math, every transaction is cryptographically logged and stored safely on the school's local hardware."*

## 📚 Data Sources & References

1. **School Electrification Deficit**:
   - **UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS)**: *Global Education Monitoring (GEM) Infrastructure Dataset*. Regional reporting indicates that approximately **35% of primary schools** in Sub-Saharan Africa have access to electricity, leaving approximately **65% un-electrified**. In rural zones and countries such as Sierra Leone or Niger, primary school electrification falls below 10%.
2. **Pedagogical Internet & School Connectivity**:
   - **UNICEF & ITU Giga Initiative**: *Global School Connectivity Mapping Report*. Over 80% of primary and secondary schools in rural Sub-Saharan Africa lack pedagogical internet connectivity. In East and Southern Africa, 88% of school-age children (ages 3–17) have no internet access at home.
3. **Child Welfare & Educational Disruption**:
   - **UNESCO**: *Out-of-School Children and Educational Continuity in Sub-Saharan Africa*. Administrative tracking errors and informal fee disputes remain a recognized driver of temporary student exclusion and dropout among vulnerable learners.
