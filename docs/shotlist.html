Global Style Prefix (Applies to all Seedance Prompts) 
Style: 8K IMAX Documentary. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no CGI artifacts.
Lighting: Natural light only — soft morning sunlight through windows, gentle atmospheric haze (10% density). No artificial lightning, no harsh neon.
Color: 60:30:10 — warm ochre earth / slate charcoal / subtle orange accent.

Technical: 24fps smooth motion, 16:9 widescreen, no jitter.
Audio: Diegetic natural environmental sound only (paper rustle, footsteps, distant schoolyard chatter). No music, no subtitles.
