# FeesFoundry User Guide

Welcome to FeesFoundry. This guide will help office staff and administrators navigate the system to manage students, record payments, and generate financial reports.

## 1. Initial Setup
When you launch FeesFoundry for the first time, you will be greeted by the **Setup Wizard**.
1. Enter your **School Name**.
2. Upload your **School Logo** (PNG/SVG/JPG).
3. Create the **Master Administrator Account** (username and password).
4. Define the current **Academic Year** (e.g., 2026).
5. Set up your **Terms** and their respective start/end dates.

## 2. Dashboard
The Dashboard is your financial command center.
- **Total Expected Fees:** Shows the total amount invoiced for the current academic year.
- **Total Collected:** Shows actual cash received.
- **Outstanding Balance:** Shows what is still owed to the school.
- **Recent Activity:** An audit log showing who recorded payments or added students recently.

## 3. Managing Students
Navigate to the **Student Accounts** tab on the left sidebar.

### Adding a Student
1. Click **"Add Student"** in the top right.
2. Fill in the student's details, guardian information, and select their Grade.
3. The system will automatically generate a unique Student ID and calculate their required fees based on their grade.

### Viewing a Student Profile & Printing Statements
1. Click on any student row in the table.
2. A detailed **Statement of Account** will appear on the right.
3. You can see their total invoiced amount, total paid, and current arrears.
4. Click **"Print Statement"** to generate a physical copy for the parent/guardian.

## 4. Recording Payments
1. Open a student's profile (as described above).
2. Click **"Record Payment"**.
3. Enter the amount paid by the guardian.
4. Confirm the transaction.
5. The system will automatically update their balance, log the transaction, and generate a printable **Receipt**.

## 5. Generating Reports
On the **Student Accounts** page, you can filter the view to generate specific reports:
- **List Students Owing:** Click the "Owing" filter button. The table will instantly update to show only students in arrears. Click "Print Overview" to print this list.
- **List Students Fully Paid:** Click the "Paid in Full" filter button.
- **Grade Reports:** Click on a specific Grade chip (e.g., "Grade 1") to filter the list and view financial performance for that grade only.

## 6. Settings & Security
- **Activity Logs:** All actions are permanently recorded. Administrators can view these logs to track who recorded specific payments.
- **Data Backup:** Because FeesFoundry works offline, your data is stored locally. It is highly recommended to regularly back up the `data.db` file located in your computer's AppData/Application Support folder.
